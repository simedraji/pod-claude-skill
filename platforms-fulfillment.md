# Platforms & Fulfillment Providers (2026)

## 1. Marketplaces (sell where buyers already search)

| Marketplace | Best for | Notes 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | Handmade/POD niche gifts, evergreen identity-based products | Largest organic-search POD buyer pool; fee structure in `marketplace-fees-affiliate.md` |
| **Amazon (Merch by Amazon)** | Apparel at scale, leveraging Amazon's existing traffic | No upfront listing cost; royalty-based; strict trademark/IP enforcement |
| **eBay** | Higher-percentage commission but more listing flexibility; good secondary channel | |
| **Walmart Marketplace / Target Plus** | US big-box reach; vetting required, less POD-native | |
| **TikTok Shop** | Social-commerce/affiliate-driven impulse buys | See `marketplace-fees-affiliate.md` for commission & fulfillment mandate |

## 2. Dedicated POD print/production platforms

These connect directly to your store (Shopify/Etsy/WooCommerce) and auto-fulfill orders.

| Platform | Strength | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Printify** | Widest product catalog, multiple supplier choice per product, cheapest base prices — best for testing/experimenting and niche products no one else offers | Quality/shipping time varies by supplier; premium plan ($29/mo) needed for best margins; doesn't manage out-of-stock automatically |
| **Printful** | Most consistent quality, easiest to use, owns more of its production (fewer surprises), strong branding tools (custom packaging, inserts) | Pricier base costs than Printify |
| **Gelato** | 140+ print partners in 30+ countries → fastest, most local, lowest-carbon shipping; strongest for EU/UK and global sellers; Personalization Studio for live customization | Less curated/branded experience than Printful |
| **Merch by Amazon** | Built-in Amazon traffic | Royalty model, strict approval/IP rules |
| **Redbubble / TeePublic / Spring / Spreadshirt / Zazzle / Society6 / Threadless / Displate / Fine Art America / Design By Humans / CafePress / Bonfire** | "Upload and forget" marketplaces with their own built-in audience — good for passive/volume design uploads, lower margin per sale than running your own Shopify store | Little control over pricing/branding; algorithm-dependent traffic |

**2026 decision rule of thumb** (confirmed by multiple comparisons): *Printify if testing/cheapest/most niche options; Printful if branding & consistent quality matter most; Gelato if global shipping speed and sustainability are the priority — especially EU/UK buyers.* None of these platforms do SEO, keyword research, or video content for you; that's still 100% the seller's job — important to repeat in PODAcademy lessons since beginners often think the print platform "does the marketing."

## 3. Dropship / fulfillment-only suppliers (mostly for TikTok Shop / Shopify dropship-style POD, not the classic Etsy "upload a design" model)

| Provider | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Plusbase** | TikTok Shop-focused fulfillment/dropship platform (sourcing + fast US delivery for TikTok Shop sellers). **Important 2026 change**: as of March 31, 2026, TikTok mandates that US local sellers route fulfillment through TikTok-managed logistics (Fulfilled by TikTok / Upgraded TikTok Shipping / Collections by TikTok) — this affects how Plusbase-style external fulfillment integrates; always verify current TikTok Shop logistics policy before teaching this as a fixed workflow, since TikTok has paused/adjusted this mandate at least once already in 2026. |
| **Merchize, ShineOn, Gooten, CustomCat, Apliiq, Gelato (also POD), JetPrint, InterestPrint, Yoycol, T-Pop, AOP+, Prodigi** | Alternative fulfillment networks, useful for product categories or regions a primary platform doesn't cover well (e.g., jewelry via ShineOn, EU production via T-Pop/Gelato) |

## 4. Ecommerce store builders (your own branded storefront)

Shopify (dominant for POD + TikTok Shop integration), WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Ecwid, Wix Stores, Squarespace Commerce. Shopify is the most common pairing with Printify/Printful/Gelato and has native TikTok Shop sync apps.

## 5. Choosing a stack — quick framework to teach beginners

1. **Where's your buyer?** Organic search intent (gifts, niche identity) → Etsy. Impulse/video-driven → TikTok Shop. Brand-building long-term → own Shopify store.
2. **Who prints it?** Pick based on the table above (catalog breadth vs. quality consistency vs. shipping speed).
3. **Who fulfills it if it's a TikTok Shop dropship model?** Check current TikTok logistics mandate before recommending Plusbase or any 3PL — this rule has changed multiple times in 2026, so verify with a quick search if it's a live teaching session.
4. **Multi-channel**: most serious sellers run Etsy + their own Shopify store + TikTok Shop simultaneously, syncing inventory/fulfillment via Printify/Printful's native multi-channel integrations.
