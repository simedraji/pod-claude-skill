# SEO & Marketing for POD (2026)

## Etsy SEO / Algorithm (2026)

Etsy ranking happens in **two stages**:

1. **Query matching** (qualification round): Etsy scans title, tags, category, and attributes for relevance to what the buyer typed.
2. **Context-specific ranking** (who wins the page): driven by **Listing Quality Score** — a behavioral score built from click-through rate, favorites, and conversion rate — plus newer 2026 factors below.

### What's new/heavier-weighted in 2026
- **Dwell time**: how long a shopper stays on your listing after clicking. Quick bounce = signal the listing wasn't relevant; longer stay (reading description, watching a listing video) = ranking boost. → Add a short listing video (Etsy allows up to ~100MB) and a genuinely useful description, not just keyword stuffing.
- **Shipping price as a direct ranking factor** (Feb 2026 update): Etsy compares your shipping cost to the category average and penalizes listings priced above it — this rewards realistic/low/free shipping baked smartly into pricing.
- **Title length cap**: titles over ~70 characters now face an algorithmic/mobile-visibility penalty — front-load your primary keyword in the **first 40 characters**.
- **Full photo coverage**: fill all 10 photo slots; first photo should be a clean, uncluttered product/lifestyle shot (it's doing double duty — human-facing AND a CTR signal to the algorithm).
- **Star Seller status** is weighted more heavily as a trust signal in 2026.
- **Semantic/intent matching** has improved — exact-keyword-stuffing matters less than it used to; natural, intent-matching phrasing performs better than robotic repetition.

### 2026 title structure to teach
`[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Keyword + modifier] | [Occasion/Recipient Keyword]` — primary keyword phrase in the first 40 characters, full title under ~70 characters.

### Tag strategy
Use all 13 tags; mix broad + long-tail + occasion/recipient phrases; avoid pure duplication of the title — tags should capture variations a buyer might type that the title doesn't cover.

### Research tools (use to find real search volume, not guesses)
**EverBee, eRank, Marmalead, Alura, Koalanda** — Etsy-specific keyword/listing analytics (search volume, competition, top-seller benchmarking). **Helium 10 / Jungle Scout** — Amazon-equivalent tools if teaching the Merch by Amazon module.

## Pinterest

Pinterest remains a top organic traffic driver for POD (especially home decor, apparel, gifts). Core playbook: keyword-rich pin titles/descriptions (same intent-matching logic as Etsy SEO), consistent daily pinning, fresh pins linking directly to listings, seasonal boards built 30–45 days ahead of the holiday/occasion.

## TikTok (organic + Shop)

- Native shoppable video/LIVE content converts far better than static image slideshows — TikTok's 2026 Content Posting Limit (CPL) algorithmically suppresses low-effort/spammy posting, rewarding genuine video performance over volume.
- For affiliate-driven TikTok Shop sales, the creator's hook in the first 1–2 seconds and a clear on-screen product tag matter more than production value.
- Pair organic content with the affiliate program (see `marketplace-fees-affiliate.md`) — most successful TikTok Shop sellers run both simultaneously.

## Facebook / Instagram

Mainly used for retargeting and paid ads layered on top of organic Etsy/Pinterest/TikTok traffic; not usually the first channel to teach a beginner POD seller, but useful as a "scale" module once a winning product is validated.
