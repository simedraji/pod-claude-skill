# Marketplace Fees & Affiliate Programs (2026)

## Etsy fee structure (2026)

Every Etsy sale carries **three mandatory fees**, plus optional advertising:

1. **Listing fee**: $0.20 per item, charged when listed AND every 4 months on renewal (or whenever it sells/renews). Listings never expire but auto-renew and re-charge every 4 months if unsold.
2. **Transaction fee**: **6.5%** of the *total amount the buyer pays* — this includes item price + shipping + gift wrapping + personalization fee (NOT sales tax for US sellers). This is the detail beginners miss: "free shipping" baked into the item price still gets the 6.5% cut, same as charging shipping separately.
3. **Payment processing fee**: ~**3% + $0.25** for US sellers (varies by country — e.g. UK ~4% + £0.20, Canada ~3% + CAD $0.25). Applies to total buyer payment including tax that Etsy collects/remits, but the 6.5% transaction fee does NOT apply to the tax portion.
4. **Offsite Ads fee (optional but often mandatory)**: 12–15% commission on sales that came from an Etsy-placed ad (Google/FB/IG/Pinterest). **Mandatory** for any shop earning over $10,000/year in the past 12 months — sellers can't opt out once they cross that threshold.
5. **Etsy Plus subscription**: optional, $10/month, bundled credits/perks.

### Worked example (illustrative, June 2026 figures)
- $30 item + $5 shipping = $35 total sale
- Listing fee: $0.20
- Transaction fee: 6.5% × $35 = $2.28
- Payment processing: 3% × $35 + $0.25 ≈ $1.30
- **Total mandatory fees ≈ $3.78 (≈10.8% effective rate)** before any ads
- If the sale came via Offsite Ads at 15%: +$5.25 → effective rate jumps to ~25.8%

**Teaching takeaway for PODAcademy**: tell زبائن/students to price with an 11–15% baseline fee buffer (more if their shop is large enough to be forced into Offsite Ads), and to never assume "free shipping" avoids the transaction fee — it doesn't.

### New-seller tip (verify before teaching as fixed)
Some 2026 sources mention a "first 40 listings free" promo via referral links for brand-new Etsy shops — confirm this is still live before promising it to students, these promos rotate.

## TikTok Shop affiliate program (2026)

- **Commission rates**: typically **5–30%** of order value depending on category (toys/beauty highest, electronics lowest); brand-set rate, paid only on completed (non-returned) sales.
- Two models: **Open Collaboration** (any eligible creator can grab the product at the seller's posted rate, no approval needed) and **Targeted/Closed Collaboration** (invite specific creators, often with a negotiated rate or hybrid flat-fee + commission deal).
- **Real cost is higher than the sticker rate**: after TikTok's ~6% platform/referral fee, returns, and Shipping Fee Program (SFP) service fee (~6% if enrolled), a nominal 15–20% commission often becomes a 25–35% effective cost of revenue. Always model this with the seller/student before they set a commission rate.
- **Settlement**: commissions paid out automatically ~14–15 days after delivery (longer, up to 31 days, for newer "introductory" sellers); no invoicing needed, deducted directly from seller payout.
- **2026 fulfillment mandate**: TikTok pushed to require all US local sellers to use TikTok-managed logistics (Fulfilled by TikTok, Upgraded Shipping, Collections by TikTok) starting late Feb–March 2026, removing the option to use outside 3PLs/Amazon MCF/direct carrier shipping. This policy has been paused/adjusted at least once in 2026 — **always verify current status with a quick search before teaching it as a hard rule**, since it directly impacts any Plusbase-based dropship workflow.
- TikTok Shop affiliate-driven sales now represent the majority of US platform GMV (reported ~42–70% depending on source/category), making it the single largest growth channel to teach alongside Etsy.

## Other affiliate networks worth knowing for course content
Amazon Associates, Impact, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, Awin — useful if students want to combine POD store traffic with affiliate income on the side, but TikTok Shop's native in-app checkout converts far higher (no external link friction) than these.

## Practical pricing framework to teach

1. Start from your **target take-home margin** (e.g., 30–40% after COGS).
2. Add the **platform's mandatory fee load** (Etsy ≈ 11–15% baseline; TikTok Shop ≈ 20–35% effective once commission + platform fee + returns are counted).
3. Add **COGS** (print + base product + shipping from supplier).
4. Back-solve the retail price. Never set commission/ad spend off a competitor's number alone — always work backward from your own unit economics.
