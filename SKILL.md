## Quick facts to keep top-of-mind (June 2026 snapshot)

- **Etsy fees**: $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction (on item+shipping+gift wrap) + ~3%+$0.25 payment processing (US) + optional 12–15% offsite ads (mandatory above $10k/yr sales). Effective fee load is usually 11–20% before ads. See `marketplace-fees-affiliate.md` for full math and per-country rates.
- **Etsy ranking in 2026** now weights *dwell time*, click-through, and conversion heavily, plus shipping price relative to category average is a direct ranking factor — cheap/free shipping matters more than ever. Title front-loading (first 40 chars) and full 10-photo coverage still matter.
- **TikTok Shop**: affiliate commissions typically run 5–30% by category (beauty/toys highest, electronics lowest); average effective US commission ~13%, but after TikTok's 6% referral fee + returns the real cost often lands near 25–35% of GMV. As of March 31, 2026, US sellers must route fulfillment through TikTok-managed logistics (FBT) — this directly affects any Plusbase/dropship workflow taught in PODAcademy, so flag it explicitly when teaching this module.
- **POD print partner choice in 2026**: Printify = widest catalog/cheapest, best for testing; Printful = best brand/quality consistency; Gelato = best for global/EU shipping speed and sustainability messaging. None of them do SEO or marketing for you — that's the seller's job (a good teaching point for PODAcademy).
- **Top niches 2026**: evergreen = pets (breed-specific), professions, faith, hobbies, family/life-milestones; trending/seasonal = mental health awareness, eco-friendly, activism, gaming, sports tied to live events. Micro-niche ("Identity × Interest" combos) consistently outperforms generic broad niches.
